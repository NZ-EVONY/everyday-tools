How GST is worked out in New Zealand

GST is 15% of the price before GST. To add it, multiply by 1.15. To take it out of a GST-inclusive price, divide by 1.15. That is the same as taking 3/23 of the total, which is why GST is about 13% of a GST-inclusive price, not 15%.

Add GST: GST = price × 15% total = price + GST
Remove GST: GST = total × 3 ÷ 23 price = total − GST

Common mistakes

  • Taking 15% off a GST-inclusive price. That removes too much. Divide by 1.15 instead.
  • Rounding every line, then adding. Rounding once on the total avoids drifting by a few cents.
  • Treating every sale as taxable. Some goods and services are zero-rated or exempt, so check the rules for yours.

What this calculator does not do

It does not decide whether you should charge GST, file a return or claim a credit. It also does not store anything: what you type stays in your browser and is gone when you close the tab.

Questions people ask

How do I take GST out of a price?

Divide the GST-inclusive price by 1.15. For $115.00 that gives $100.00, so $15.00 of the total was GST.

Why is my total a cent different from my invoice?

Software rounds GST at different points. This calculator works out GST on the sum of all lines and rounds once, half a cent up.

Do all goods and services carry GST?

No. Some supplies are zero-rated, such as many exports, and some are exempt, such as residential rent. Check Inland Revenue guidance for your situation.

Is anything I enter saved or sent?

No. The calculation runs in your browser and nothing is stored or sent anywhere.
